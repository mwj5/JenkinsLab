  <!-- Core Research Pillars -->
  <section id="research" class="py-20 max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8">
    <div class="max-w-3xl mb-14">
      <h2 class="text-sm font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-cwruBlue mb-2">Research Programs</h2>
      <h3 class="text-3xl sm:text-4xl font-extrabold text-slate-900">Four Core Research Pillars</h3>
      <p class="mt-4 text-slate-600 text-lg">
        Our interdisciplinary team bridges optical physics, neural engineering, computational reconstruction, and multi-omic bioinformatics to translate optical discoveries into clinical diagnostics and therapeutics.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-8">
      <!-- Pillar 1 -->
      <div class="bg-white rounded-2xl p-8 shadow-sm border border-slate-200 flex flex-col justify-between">
        <div>
          <span class="inline-block px-3 py-1 rounded-md bg-sky-100 text-cwruNavy text-xs font-bold mb-4">Pillar 01 &bull; Pain &amp; Spinal Cord Injury</span>
          <h4 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-900 mb-3">
            Photobiomodulation (PBM), tSNIP &amp; Neural Recovery
          </h4>
          <p class="text-slate-600 leading-relaxed mb-4">
            We developed <strong>Transient Selective Neural Inhibition via Photobiomodulation (tSNIP)</strong>, a non-pharmacological therapy that selectively inhibits small-diameter nociceptive sensory fibers (C and A&delta; fibers) while preserving large-fiber motor function.
          </p>
          <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-slate-700 list-disc pl-5">
            <li>Establishing irradiance and dose-response benchmarks for chronic neuropathic pain management.</li>
            <li>Investigating 808 nm photobiomodulation in spinal cord injury (SCI) models, targeting mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase activation, nitric oxide dissociation, and ATP restoration.</li>
            <li>Analyzing impactor biomechanics and functional recovery trajectories in preclinical neurotrauma.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Pillar 2 -->
      <div class="bg-white rounded-2xl p-8 shadow-sm border border-slate-200 flex flex-col justify-between">
        <div>
          <span class="inline-block px-3 py-1 rounded-md bg-sky-100 text-cwruNavy text-xs font-bold mb-4">Pillar 02 &bull; Autonomic Bioelectronic Medicine</span>
          <h4 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-900 mb-3">
            Infrared Neuromodulation (IRN) &amp; Circuit Mapping
          </h4>
          <p class="text-slate-600 leading-relaxed mb-4">
            Modulating the autonomic nervous system offers transformative potential for treating hypertension, heart failure, arrhythmias, and sleep apnea. We use pulsed infrared light to stimulate or block neural activity with spatial precision unachievable by electrodes.
          </p>
          <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-slate-700 list-disc pl-5">
            <li>Preferential recruitment and reversible block of small-diameter autonomic and sensory axons.</li>
            <li>Targeted optical interrogation of the nodose ganglion and vagus nerve to decode organ-specific control circuits.</li>
            <li>3D microCT fascicular segmentation and molecular mapping of peripheral ganglia.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Pillar 3 -->
      <div class="bg-white rounded-2xl p-8 shadow-sm border border-slate-200 flex flex-col justify-between">
        <div>
          <span class="inline-block px-3 py-1 rounded-md bg-sky-100 text-cwruNavy text-xs font-bold mb-4">Pillar 03 &bull; 3D Microscopy &amp; Computation</span>
          <h4 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-900 mb-3">
            Advanced 3D Optical Microscopy, Tissue Clearing &amp; Reconstruction
          </h4>
          <p class="text-slate-600 leading-relaxed mb-4">
            We build high-speed optical imaging systems, rapid chemical clearing protocols, and machine-learning reconstruction pipelines to visualize intact 3D tissues without destructive sectioning.
          </p>
          <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-slate-700 list-disc pl-5">
            <li><strong>LIMPID Optical Clearing:</strong> Single-step lipid-preserving clearing compatible with whole-mount RNA FISH and immunohistochemistry.</li>
            <li><strong>SLIME &amp; Light-Sheet / MUSE:</strong> High-speed 3D microvascular mapping and rapid intraoperative margin assessment.</li>
            <li><strong>Computational Optics:</strong> 1-bit SPAD quanta video reconstruction (<em>bit2bit</em>) and 3D/4D Gaussian Splatting for deep-tissue focal stack reconstruction.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Pillar 4 -->
      <div class="bg-white rounded-2xl p-8 shadow-sm border border-slate-200 flex flex-col justify-between">
        <div>
          <span class="inline-block px-3 py-1 rounded-md bg-sky-100 text-cwruNavy text-xs font-bold mb-4">Pillar 04 &bull; Ophthalmology &amp; Cardiology</span>
          <h4 class="text-2xl font-bold text-slate-900 mb-3">
            Ocular Surface Neurobiology &amp; Developmental Cardiology
          </h4>
          <p class="text-slate-600 leading-relaxed mb-4">
            Our multimodal optical coherence tomography (OCT) and fluorescence platforms enable non-invasive functional imaging of corneal nerves and the embryonic heart.
          </p>
          <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-slate-700 list-disc pl-5">
            <li><strong>Ocular Surface Neurobiology:</strong> <em>In vivo</em> calcium and structural imaging of corneal nerves in evaporative dry eye disease and peripheral neuropathy.</li>
            <li><strong>Optical Pacing &amp; 4D OCT:</strong> Pioneered non-invasive optical pacing of the embryonic heart (<em>Nature Photonics</em>) and 4D gated Doppler OCT hemodynamics.</li>
            <li><strong>Congenital Heart Defects:</strong> Integrating 3D OCT phenotyping with DNA methylation (SeSAMe/DMR) and transcriptomic profiling to uncover mechanisms of prenatal alcohol-induced heart defects.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

As Director of the CWRU School of Medicine Light Microscopy Imaging Core, he leads institutional initiatives in cleared-tissue light-sheet microscopy, super-resolution imaging, and spatial transcriptomics.
